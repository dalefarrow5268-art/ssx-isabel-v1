# SSX Division 00 Activities — Bidding and Contract Requirements

Potential activities under the traditional 1995 Division 00 structure. Use only activities applicable to the project delivery and procurement method.

## 00020 — Invitation to Bid

- Confirm project scope and bid packages
- Confirm bidder qualification requirements
- Confirm issue date and bid due date
- Confirm prebid meeting and site visit
- Prepare invitation to bid
- Approve bidder list
- Publish or distribute invitation
- Confirm bidder receipt
- Track bidding/not bidding responses
- Add replacement bidders
- Issue reminder notices
- Close invitation period

## 00100 — Instructions to Bidders

- Draft bidding instructions
- Define electronic or sealed bid procedure
- Define questions and substitution deadlines
- Define addendum cutoff
- Define bid-security requirements
- Define alternates, allowances and unit prices
- Define acknowledgment requirements
- Define bid-validity period
- Conduct legal and owner review
- Issue final instructions
- Respond to procedural questions
- Document approved exceptions

## 00200 — Information Available to Bidders

- Assemble surveys
- Assemble geotechnical information
- Assemble environmental reports
- Assemble hazardous-material reports
- Assemble existing-condition information
- Assemble utility information
- Assemble record documents
- Establish disclaimer and reliance terms
- Upload bidder information
- Confirm readable files
- Issue revised information
- Record bidder access
- Preserve issued package

## 00300 — Bid Forms

- Prepare base bid form
- Prepare trade or package breakdown
- Prepare alternates
- Prepare allowances
- Prepare unit prices
- Prepare subcontractor listing
- Prepare schedule and duration acknowledgment
- Prepare addenda acknowledgment
- Prepare signature and authority fields
- Review form completeness
- Issue bid form
- Receive bid
- Conduct completeness check
- Request permitted clarification
- Preserve original submission

## 00400 — Bid Supplements

- Prepare bid bond form
- Prepare qualifications statement
- Prepare experience requirements
- Prepare proposed subcontractor list
- Prepare manpower and schedule information
- Prepare safety and quality history request
- Prepare insurance information
- Prepare financial-capacity information
- Receive supplements
- Review completeness
- Verify references
- Conduct bidder interview
- Resolve qualifications
- Approve responsive bidder

## 00500 — Agreement

- Select contract form
- Insert parties and project information
- Insert contract sum
- Insert contract time and milestones
- Insert liquidated-damages provisions when applicable
- Insert retainage and payment terms
- Incorporate alternates and allowances
- Incorporate accepted clarifications
- Conduct legal review
- Conduct owner review
- Issue for signature
- Contractor signs
- Owner signs
- Distribute executed agreement
- Record effective date

## 00600 — Bonds and Certificates

- Confirm performance-bond requirement
- Confirm payment-bond requirement
- Confirm bid bond
- Confirm consent of surety
- Obtain insurance certificates
- Verify coverage and limits
- Verify additional insured requirements
- Verify builder’s risk
- Verify professional liability when applicable
- Review bond forms
- Review surety authorization
- Correct deficiencies
- Obtain final bonds
- Obtain final certificates
- Release notice to proceed prerequisite

## 00700 — General Conditions

- Select general conditions
- Review owner-contractor responsibilities
- Review architect/engineer authority
- Review payment procedure
- Review changes procedure
- Review claims and dispute procedure
- Review time and delay provisions
- Review inspections and correction
- Review termination and suspension
- Review insurance and bonds
- Review substantial and final completion
- Coordinate with agreement
- Coordinate with supplementary conditions
- Approve and issue general conditions

## 00800 — Supplementary Conditions

- Identify project-specific modifications
- Modify owner requirements
- Modify insurance and bond requirements
- Define scheduling requirements
- Define payment and lien-waiver requirements
- Define background screening and site access
- Define prevailing wage or workforce reporting
- Define sustainability requirements
- Define electronic document system
- Define testing and inspection responsibility
- Define owner-furnished information or equipment
- Define weather and delay procedures
- Conduct legal review
- Issue final supplementary conditions

## 00900 — Addenda and Modifications

- Receive bidder question
- Determine need for addendum
- Prepare drawing revision
- Prepare specification revision
- Prepare bid-form revision
- Coordinate all disciplines
- Obtain owner approval
- Issue addendum
- Confirm bidder receipt
- Extend bid date when required
- Require acknowledgment
- Evaluate bid using issued addenda
- Issue post-bid clarification when permitted
- Preserve final procurement record

## Division 00 procurement milestones

- Bid documents approved
- Bid documents issued
- Prebid conference complete
- Bidder questions due
- Final addendum issued
- Bids due
- Bid leveling complete
- Scope interviews complete
- Recommendation issued
- Owner award approved
- Bonds and insurance accepted
- Agreement executed
- Notice to proceed issued

## Typical schedule logic

- Procurement strategy → bid-package preparation
- Approved documents → invitation to bid
- Invitation → prebid meeting and questions
- Questions → addenda
- Final addendum → bid submission
- Bids → leveling and scope review
- Recommendation → owner approval
- Approval → agreement, bonds and insurance
- Executed agreement and prerequisites → notice to proceed
