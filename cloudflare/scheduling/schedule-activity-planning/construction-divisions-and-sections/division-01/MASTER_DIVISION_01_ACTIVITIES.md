## Typical schedule logic

- Executed contract → administrative setup
- Administrative setup → mobilization
- Baseline schedule and submittal register → controlled execution
- Approved submittal and ready workfront → installation
- Installation → inspection/testing
- Accepted installation → startup/commissioning
- Commissioning and punch → substantial completion
- Closeout submittals and final corrections → final completion
- Final acceptance → warranty and post-occupancy activities

## Required SSX controls

- Division 01 activities must link to the technical sections they govern.
- Project-wide recurring activities need explicit calendars and responsible owners.
- Payment, schedule, submittal and change cycles use contract dates.
- Temporary facilities include installation, maintenance, relocation and removal.
- Quality and special inspections occur before concealment.
- Closeout begins during procurement and installation, not at project end.
- Commissioning, training and record documents are tied to systems and turnover packages.
- Exact project specifications govern section applicability and titles.
