# SSX 2026 CSI Division 02 Activities — Existing Conditions

Potential schedule activities for documenting, investigating, protecting, removing, relocating and remediating existing construction and site conditions.

> Existing-condition work is highly project-specific. Approved surveys, hazardous-material reports, permits, abatement plans and field conditions govern. Stop and notify the responsible parties when unknown or suspect conditions are encountered.

## Typical schedule logic

- Records review → survey and assessment
- Assessment → sampling and investigation
- Results → design, permits and work plan
- Hazardous-material clearance → demolition
- Utility disconnect → demolition/removal
- Removal → verification/clearance testing
- Accepted clearance → new construction
- Remediation → confirmation sampling
- Regulatory acceptance → backfill/restoration
- Final records → Division 02 closeout

## Required SSX controls

- Link every removal activity to the exact area and successor work.
- Complete hazardous-material assessment before disturbing suspect materials.
- Separate utility shutdown, disconnect, removal and restoration activities.
- Include containment setup, monitoring, clearance and teardown.
- Track salvage separately from demolition waste.
- Preserve manifests, disposal receipts and chain-of-custody records.
- Identify hold points for owner, consultant and regulator review.
- Record unknown conditions immediately and connect them to change management.
- Protect occupied areas and adjacent property.
- Do not release areas until required clearance and acceptance are documented.
