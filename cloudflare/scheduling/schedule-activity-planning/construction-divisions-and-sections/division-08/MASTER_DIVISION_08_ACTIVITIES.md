# SSX 2026 CSI Division 08 — Openings Activity List

## 08 01 00 — Maintenance of Openings

- Survey existing openings
- Document damage, leakage and operation
- Perform probes and testing
- Prepare repair plan
- Approve repair mockup
- Remove failed components
- Repair frames, sash and hardware
- Replace glazing and seals
- Refinish repaired work
- Test operation and water resistance
- Complete punch and closeout

## 08 05 00 — Common Work Results for Openings

- Conduct openings coordination conference
- Prepare coordinated opening schedule
- Submit product data and certifications
- Submit shop drawings
- Coordinate rough openings, blocking and embeds
- Coordinate fire, smoke, security and accessibility requirements
- Approve samples and mockups
- Procure materials
- Receive, store and protect materials
- Complete preparatory quality-control meeting
- Document concealed anchors

## 08 06 00 — Schedules for Openings

- Reconcile architectural opening schedules
- Verify opening numbers and ratings
- Coordinate doors, frames, hardware and glazing
- Resolve schedule conflicts
- Issue approved opening schedule
- Release procurement packages
- Track revisions
- Verify installed assemblies
- Update record schedule
