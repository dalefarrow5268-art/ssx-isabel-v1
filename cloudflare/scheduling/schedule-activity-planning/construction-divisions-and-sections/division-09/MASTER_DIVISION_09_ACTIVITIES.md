# SSX 2026 CSI Division 09 — Finishes Activity List

## 09 01 00 — Maintenance of Finishes

- Survey existing finishes
- Document damage, staining, delamination and moisture
- Perform adhesion and material testing
- Prepare repair plan
- Construct repair mockup
- Remove failed finishes
- Repair substrates
- Patch or replace finish materials
- Match color, pattern and texture
- Clean and protect repaired work
- Inspect repairs
- Complete punch and closeout

## 09 05 00 — Common Work Results for Finishes

- Conduct finishes coordination conference
- Prepare finish schedule
- Submit product data and certifications
- Submit samples and color selections
- Construct room or finish mockups
- Verify environmental requirements
- Coordinate substrates, blocking and penetrations
- Procure finish materials
- Receive, store and acclimate materials
- Complete preparatory quality-control meeting
- Protect adjacent and completed work

## 09 06 00 — Schedules for Finishes

- Reconcile finish plans and schedules
- Verify room finish designations
- Coordinate material transitions
- Coordinate colors, patterns and orientations
- Resolve schedule conflicts
- Issue approved finish schedule
- Release finish procurement packages
- Track finish substitutions and revisions
- Verify installed finishes by room
- Update record finish schedule
