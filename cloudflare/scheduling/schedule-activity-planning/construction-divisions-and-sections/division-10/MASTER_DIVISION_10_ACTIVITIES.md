## 10 01 00 — Maintenance of Specialties

- Survey existing specialty items
- Document damage, missing components and operation
- Verify original materials and mounting

- Approve repair sample

- Repair substrates and anchors
- Repair or replace specialty items

- Test operation
- Clean and protect repaired work
- Complete punch and closeout
