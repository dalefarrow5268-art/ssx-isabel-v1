# SSX 2026 CSI Division 12 — Furnishings Activity List

## 12 01 00 — Maintenance of Furnishings

- Survey existing furnishings
- Document damage, wear and missing components
- Verify manufacturer and finish information
- Prepare repair or replacement plan
- Approve repair sample
- Remove damaged components
- Repair substrates and supports
- Repair or replace furnishings
- Match colors, patterns and finishes
- Adjust movable components
- Clean and protect repaired work
- Complete punch and closeout

- Submit product data and certifications

- Procure furnishings
- Receive, inventory and protect materials
- Complete preparatory quality-control review

- Protect completed furnishings

## 12 05 13 — Fabrics

- Clean and protect fabric
- Replace stained or damaged material

## 12 06 00 — Schedules for Furnishings

- Verify installed furnishings by location
- Update record furnishing schedule
