# SSX 2026 CSI Division 13 — Special Construction Activity List

## 13 01 00 — Maintenance of Special Construction

- Survey existing special construction
- Document deterioration, leakage and operational problems
- Perform probes and performance testing
- Prepare repair and restoration plan
- Approve repair mockup
- Isolate and protect occupied areas
- Remove failed components
- Repair structure, enclosure and systems
- Replace damaged specialty components
- Test repaired systems
- Complete regulatory inspection
- Submit closeout records

## 13 05 00 — Common Work Results for Special Construction

- Conduct special-construction coordination conference

- Prepare coordinated shop drawings
- Coordinate structure, enclosure, utilities and controls
- Approve materials, finishes and mockups
- Plan long-lead procurement and fabrication
- Plan delivery, rigging and temporary works
- Verify foundations, embeds and supports
- Complete preparatory quality-control meeting
- Coordinate specialty inspections and testing
- Protect completed work

## 13 08 00 — Commissioning of Special Construction

- Verify installation readiness

- Start up specialty systems
- Test normal operating modes
- Test alarms, safeties and emergency modes
- Document deficiencies
- Correct and retest systems
- Train owner personnel
- Submit final commissioning report
