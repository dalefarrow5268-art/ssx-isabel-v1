# SSX 2026 CSI Division 14 — Conveying Equipment Activity List

## 14 01 00 — Maintenance of Conveying Equipment

- Survey existing conveying equipment
- Review maintenance and callback records
- Perform condition and code assessment
- Document worn, obsolete and unsafe components
- Prepare modernization or repair plan
- Submit shutdown and phasing plan
- Provide temporary access or service
- Repair or replace equipment
- Upgrade controls and safety devices
- Perform adjustment and testing
- Coordinate authority inspection
- Return equipment to service
- Submit updated maintenance records

## 14 05 00 — Common Work Results for Conveying Equipment

- Conduct conveying-equipment coordination conference
- Prepare coordinated equipment and hoistway schedule
- Submit product data, engineering and certifications
- Coordinate hoistways, pits, machine spaces and overhead clearances
- Coordinate structural supports, embeds and openings
- Coordinate power, communications, fire alarm and security interfaces
- Plan long-lead procurement
- Plan delivery, rigging and temporary use
- Complete preparatory quality-control meeting
- Coordinate inspection and acceptance requirements
- Protect equipment and finished work

## 14 08 00 — Commissioning of Conveying Equipment

- Prepare conveying-equipment commissioning plan
- Review approved sequences and interfaces
- Complete prefunctional checklists
- Verify power and emergency systems
- Start up equipment
- Test normal operation
- Test rated load and speed
- Test doors, brakes and safety devices
- Test fire-recall and emergency modes
- Test communications and security interfaces
- Document deficiencies
- Correct and retest equipment
- Coordinate authority acceptance
- Train owner personnel
- Submit final commissioning report
