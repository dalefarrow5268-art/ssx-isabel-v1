# SSX 2026 CSI Division 21 — Fire Suppression Activity List

## 21 01 00 — Operation and Maintenance of Fire Suppression

- Review existing fire-suppression drawings and inspection records
- Survey piping, valves, sprinklers and equipment
- Identify impairments, corrosion and code deficiencies
- Submit impairment and shutdown plan
- Notify owner and fire authority
- Provide temporary fire protection
- Drain and isolate affected systems
- Repair or replace deficient components
- Restore system
- Perform required testing
- Coordinate authority reinspection
- Update record drawings and inspection records
