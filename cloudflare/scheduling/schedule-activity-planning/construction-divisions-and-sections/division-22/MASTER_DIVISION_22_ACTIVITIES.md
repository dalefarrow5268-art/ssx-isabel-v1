# SSX 2026 CSI Division 22 — Plumbing Activity List

## 22 01 00 — Operation and Maintenance of Plumbing

- Review plumbing drawings and maintenance records
- Survey piping, fixtures and equipment
- Identify leaks, corrosion, blockages and code deficiencies
- Prepare shutdown and repair plan
- Notify affected occupants
- Isolate and drain systems
- Repair or replace deficient components
- Restore finishes and firestopping
- Flush and disinfect affected piping
- Test repaired systems
- Update record drawings
- Complete closeout
