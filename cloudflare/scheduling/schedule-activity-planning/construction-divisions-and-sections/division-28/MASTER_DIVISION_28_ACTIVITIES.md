# SSX 2026 CSI Division 28 — Electronic Safety and Security Activity List

## 28 01 00 — Operation and Maintenance of Electronic Safety and Security

- Review record drawings, test reports, configurations and service history
- Survey existing control panels, servers, field devices and cabling
- Document failed, obsolete and unsupported components
- Back up databases, firmware and system configurations
- Develop outage, migration and continuity-of-protection plan
- Coordinate temporary security and fire-watch measures
- Isolate and remove deficient components
- Repair, replace or upgrade equipment
- Restore programming and integrations
- Test affected devices, alarm paths and communications
- Update records, labels and asset inventory
- Train owner maintenance personnel
- Complete closeout
