## 31 01 00 — Maintenance of Earthwork

- Survey existing grades, slopes, embankments and drainage
- Document settlement, erosion, washouts and unstable areas
- Review geotechnical and maintenance records
- Establish survey-control and monitoring points

- Regrade and restore earthwork

- Compact repaired areas
- Test density and material compliance
- Restore drainage and vegetation
- Update records and closeout
