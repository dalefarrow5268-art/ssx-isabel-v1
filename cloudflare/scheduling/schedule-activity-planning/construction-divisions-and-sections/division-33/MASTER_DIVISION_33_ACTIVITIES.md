# SSX 2026 CSI Division 33 — Utilities Activity List

## 33 01 00 — Operation and Maintenance of Utilities

- Review records and survey existing operation and maintenance of utilities
- Locate active utilities and verify service responsibilities
- Document leaks, failures, corrosion, settlement and capacity deficiencies
- Coordinate utility-owner outage and continuity requirements
- Prepare repair, bypass and temporary-service plan
- Isolate, drain, de-energize or make the system safe
- Repair, clean, rehabilitate or replace deficient work
- Restore coatings, cathodic protection, labels and access
- Test repaired systems and affected interfaces
- Restore surfaces and service
- Update asset records and maintenance documentation
