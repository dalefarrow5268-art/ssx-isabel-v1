## 35 01 00 — Operation and Maintenance of Waterway and Marine Construction

- Review record drawings, prior inspections and maintenance history for operation and maintenance of waterway and marine construction
- Survey existing bathymetry, elevations, alignment and visible condition
- Inspect submerged work using divers, sonar or remotely operated equipment as applicable
- Document deterioration, scour, sedimentation, leakage, corrosion and operational deficiencies
- Coordinate navigation restrictions, water-control operations and owner access
- Obtain environmental approvals and establish in-water work windows
- Submit repair, dredging or rehabilitation procedures for operation and maintenance of waterway and marine construction
- Mobilize marine access, temporary controls and containment
- Remove obstructions, failed materials or accumulated sediment
- Repair, restore or maintain operation and maintenance of waterway and marine construction by reach, station or structure
- Perform post-work survey, inspection and operational testing
- Correct deficiencies and update maintenance and asset records
