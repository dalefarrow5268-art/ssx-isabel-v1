# SSX 2026 CSI Division 41 — Material Processing and Handling Equipment Activity List

## 41 01 00 — Operation and Maintenance of Material Processing and Handling Equipment

- Review asset records, manuals, inspections and operating history for operation and maintenance of material processing and handling equipment
- Perform condition assessment and document wear, corrosion, leakage, vibration and safety deficiencies
- Verify equipment capacity, guards, controls, foundations and utility conditions
- Develop shutdown, isolation, lockout/tagout and material-removal plan
- Coordinate confined-space, combustible-dust and stored-energy controls as applicable
- Submit cleaning, repair, alignment and replacement procedures
- Remove accumulated material and make equipment safe for work
- Repair or replace worn structural, mechanical, electrical and control components
- Realign, lubricate and restore operation and maintenance of material processing and handling equipment
- Inspect guards, emergency stops, access and housekeeping provisions
- Perform no-load and loaded operational testing
- Correct deficiencies and update maintenance and asset records

## 41 08 00 — Commissioning of Material Processing and Handling Equipment

- Verify installation, lubrication, alignment, guards and utility connections
- Confirm motor rotation, control logic, emergency stops and permissives
- Complete mechanical completion walkdown and safety inspection
- Perform static, jog and no-load functional checks
- Test local, remote, manual, automatic and emergency operating modes
- Introduce representative material and increase load in controlled steps
- Verify capacity, speed, accuracy, vibration, temperature and power consumption
- Test interlocks, alarms, blockage, overload and loss-of-power response
- Document deficiencies and complete corrective work
- Repeat failed tests and obtain owner acceptance
- Train operators and maintenance personnel
- Deliver baselines, settings, test records, manuals, spares and final report
