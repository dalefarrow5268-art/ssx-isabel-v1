# SSX 2026 CSI Division 42 — Process Heating, Cooling, and Drying Equipment Activity List

This master library lists potential schedule activities for Division 42 equipment. Select only activities applicable to the project, split work by system and physical location when useful, and verify section numbers and titles against the project specifications and the licensed/current CSI MasterFormat source.

## Scheduling controls

- Treat manufacturer engineering, code review, fabrication, factory testing, delivery, setting, connections, startup, performance testing, training, and turnover as separate activities when they affect the critical path.
- Include shutdowns, lockout/tagout, purge and cooldown, refrigerant or hazardous-material recovery, refractory cure-out, water treatment, emissions approvals, combustible-dust safeguards, and AHJ hold points where applicable.
- Record predecessor system readiness, owner operations participation, required utilities and consumables, acceptance criteria, and responsibility for every startup or test.
- Break installation and commissioning activities down by process train, equipment tag, area, elevation, batch line, or outage window rather than using one project-wide activity.
