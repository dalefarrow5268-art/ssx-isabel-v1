# SSX 2026 CSI Division 44 — Pollution and Waste Control Equipment Activity List

This master library lists potential schedule activities for Division 44 equipment. Select only activities applicable to the project, split work by system and physical location when useful, and verify section numbers and titles against the project specifications and the licensed/current CSI MasterFormat source.

## Scheduling controls

- Treat environmental permitting, baseline monitoring, process design, hazard review, manufacturer engineering, fabrication, factory testing, delivery, installation, startup, compliance testing, training, and turnover as separate activities when they affect the critical path.
- Include outages, temporary compliance controls, lockout/tagout, decontamination, regulated-waste profiling, manifests, confined-space controls, pressure testing, emissions or discharge sampling, regulatory witnesses, and AHJ hold points where applicable.
- Record design pollutant or waste characteristics, operating limits, permit conditions, required utilities and consumables, sampling methods, laboratories, chain of custody, acceptance criteria, and responsibility for every test.
- Break installation and commissioning activities down by emission point, waste stream, process train, equipment tag, area, elevation, or outage window rather than using one project-wide activity.

## 44 01 00 — Operation and Maintenance of Pollution and Waste Control Equipment

- Review permits, record drawings, equipment data, operating history, compliance reports, alarms, maintenance logs, and prior inspections for 44 01 00 Operation and Maintenance of Pollution and Waste Control Equipment
- Perform condition assessment and document corrosion, erosion, fouling, leakage, vibration, coatings, linings, refractory, media, seals, bearings, and controls
- Define outage boundaries, temporary pollution-control needs, shutdown sequence, isolation points, monitoring requirements, and return-to-service milestones
- Prepare maintenance procedures, repair submittals, waste profiles, permits, manufacturer requirements, and quality-control hold points
- Conduct pre-outage coordination meeting with operations, safety, environmental compliance, quality control, and affected trades
- Shut down, cool, drain, depressurize, purge, ventilate, decontaminate, isolate, and lock out equipment; verify zero-energy state
- Open, clean, inspect, sample, and measure accessible internals and rotating components; complete specified nondestructive examination
- Repair or replace media, filters, internals, refractory, coatings, linings, seals, bearings, drives, ductwork, piping, and instruments as applicable
- Reassemble with verified clearances, alignment, torque, gaskets, lubrication, cleanliness, material compatibility, and waste containment
- Perform pressure, vacuum, leak, rotation, vibration, electrical, calibration, alarm, permissive, emergency-stop, and interlock tests
- Complete controlled startup and verify capacity, removal efficiency, emissions or discharge quality, noise, odor, leakage, and operating stability
- Correct deficiencies, reinspect, update compliance and maintenance records, and deliver spares, warranties, and turnover documents

## 44 05 05 — Selective Demolition for Pollution and Waste Control Equipment

- Survey existing pollution-control equipment, supports, utilities, hazardous residues, retained systems, and demolition limits for 44 05 05 Selective Demolition for Pollution and Waste Control Equipment
- Confirm shutdown windows, temporary compliance controls, owner salvage requirements, decontamination standards, and regulated waste streams
- Prepare demolition, engineered-lift, isolation, purge, ventilation, environmental-control, monitoring, and permitting plans
- Identify, label, and protect equipment, ductwork, piping, controls, wiring, structures, fire barriers, and monitoring systems to remain
- Verify drain-down, depressurization, purge, decontamination, air testing, lockout/tagout, and documented zero-energy state
- Remove and containerize contaminated media, ash, dust, sludge, liquids, refractory, coatings, and residues under the approved waste profile
- Disconnect process, utility, relief, vent, drain, exhaust, power, grounding, and control connections at approved boundaries
- Dismantle, rig, transport, salvage, recycle, or dispose of equipment and materials with manifests and chain-of-custody records
- Cap, blind, seal, label, and pressure- or leak-test abandoned and retained connections
- Patch penetrations, repair substrates, containment, and fire barriers, and make the area safe for successor work
- Inspect retained systems and verify continuous environmental compliance and operability after demolition
- Complete deficiency correction, housekeeping, salvage reconciliation, disposal records, monitoring reports, and demolition closeout
