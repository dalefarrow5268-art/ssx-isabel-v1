# SSX 2026 CSI Division 45 — Industry-Specific Manufacturing Equipment Activity List

This master library lists potential schedule activities for Division 45 manufacturing equipment. Division 45 intentionally provides user-defined ranges for owner-specific production systems. Select only applicable activities, assign project-specific titles without overwriting the CSI family structure, split work by production line and location, and verify numbering against the project specifications and the licensed/current CSI MasterFormat source.

## Scheduling controls

- Treat process definition, production simulation, OEM selection, design reviews, hazard assessment, fabrication, software development, factory acceptance testing, delivery, installation, site acceptance testing, production qualification, training, and turnover as separate activities when they affect the critical path.
- Include production outages, temporary capacity, lockout/tagout, machine guarding, safety PLC validation, clean or hygienic controls, hazardous-area requirements, environmental permits, raw materials, tooling, software, network integration, and owner validation where applicable.
- Record product mix, takt or batch time, design and peak production rates, yield, scrap, quality attributes, utility demand, material and personnel flow, changeover, reliability, and acceptance criteria for every equipment package.
- Break installation and commissioning activities down by production line, cell, skid, machine tag, clean zone, area, elevation, or outage window rather than using one project-wide activity.
