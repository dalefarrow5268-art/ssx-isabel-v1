# SSX 2026 CSI Division 48 — Electrical Power Generation Activity List

This master library lists potential schedule activities for Division 48 electrical power generation. Select only activities applicable to the project, split work by generating unit, energy block, array, turbine, equipment tag, circuit, substation, plant area, and outage or energization window, and verify section numbers and titles against the project specifications and the licensed/current CSI MasterFormat source.

## Scheduling controls

- Treat resource assessment, feasibility, environmental review, utility application, interconnection studies, permitting, design, procurement, factory testing, delivery, installation, energization, synchronization, performance testing, training, and turnover as separate activities when they affect the critical path.
- Include utility or ISO approvals, power-purchase and interconnection agreements, protection studies and settings, telemetry, revenue metering, cybersecurity, grid-code compliance, energization authority, dispatch coordination, and commercial-operation milestones.
- Coordinate fuel or energy supply, emissions, water use, cooling, heat rejection, fire protection, hazardous materials, batteries, hydrogen, high voltage, arc flash, stored energy, confined spaces, rotating equipment, environmental permits, and emergency response.
- Plan outages, temporary generation, black-start requirements, islanding, anti-islanding, commissioning power, first fuel or resource admission, synchronization, load testing, reliability runs, seasonal testing, and owner operations staffing.
- Record gross and net output, energy input, efficiency or heat rate, power factor, ramp rate, turndown, redundancy, availability, power quality, emissions, environmental limits, auxiliary load, performance guarantees, and acceptance criteria for every generating package.
- Break installation and commissioning down by generating unit, block, string, turbine, skid, transformer, inverter, circuit, protection zone, SCADA boundary, and approved switching window rather than using one project-wide activity.

## Library controls

- Project specifications, approved studies, permits, utility and authority requirements, and manufacturer instructions govern final activity selection and acceptance criteria.
- Each selected activity must carry the Division 48 section, generating unit or system boundary, physical location, responsible party, predecessor, successor, planned duration, calendar, switching or outage requirement, and required inspection or test.
- Studies, permits, procurement, fabrication, factory testing, delivery, installation, inspection, energization, synchronization, performance testing, correction, training, and turnover remain separate activities when meaningful.
- First energization, first fuel or resource admission, synchronization, islanding, protection testing, reliability runs, and commercial operation require explicit owner and utility authorization milestones.
- Reserved or unused sections are not invented. Project-specific numbering is versioned and cross-referenced without silently overwriting the master library.

## Research basis

- [CSI MasterFormat 2026](https://www.csiresources.org/standards/masterformat2026) is the governing organizational standard; the project’s licensed/current edition and specifications control.
- Public MasterFormat listings, federal guide specifications, utility standards, grid codes, environmental permits, and manufacturer procedures may validate scope but do not replace project requirements.
