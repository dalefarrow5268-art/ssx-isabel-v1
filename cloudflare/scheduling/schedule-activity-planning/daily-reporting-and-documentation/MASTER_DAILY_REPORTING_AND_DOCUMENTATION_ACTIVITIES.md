# SSX Master Daily Reporting and Project Documentation Activities

Potential construction schedule activities for creating, validating, distributing, retaining and closing the contemporaneous project record from preconstruction through warranty.

> Records should be factual, timely, location-specific, searchable and supported by evidence. Project contracts, company procedures, litigation holds, privacy requirements and applicable law determine final content and retention.

## Core documentation questions

| Field | Required answer |
|---|---|
| What happened? | Work, decision, direction, delivery, inspection, test, event, delay, correction or milestone |
| When? | Date, time, shift, duration and reporting period |
| Where? | Project, building, floor, room, grid, elevation, system or workfront |
| Who? | Company, crew, responsible person, visitor, inspector, witness or decision maker |
| How much? | Manpower, hours, equipment, quantity installed, delivery quantity or measured impact |
| Why does it matter? | Progress, safety, quality, payment, change, delay, risk, acceptance or closeout |
| What proves it? | Photograph, video, ticket, checklist, test result, correspondence, signature or system record |
| What follows? | Action, notice, correction, reinspection, decision, schedule update or closure |

## Standard documentation workflow

1. Define documentation requirements
2. Establish file structure and naming
3. Assign record ownership
4. Capture information at the time of occurrence
5. Attach objective evidence
6. Verify accuracy and completeness
7. Certify or sign when required
8. Distribute to required parties
9. Create actions and notices
10. Link related records
11. Correct errors through traceable revision
12. Preserve original record
13. Apply retention and access controls
14. Transfer closeout records
15. Archive final project record

## Daily and periodic cadence

| Frequency | Typical records |
|---|---|
| Each shift | Daily report, workforce, work performed, equipment, safety, quality, delays and photographs |
| Every delivery | Delivery ticket, receiving inspection, damage, certificates and storage location |

- Inspection/test → result and release

- Completed work → record-document update
- Turnover → record transfer
- Final audit → archive

## Required schedule controls

- Complete daily reports daily, not from memory at month end.
- Identify exact work locations and responsible companies.
- Record quantities and production, not only general descriptions.
- Separate fact, observation, direction and opinion.
- Link records to schedule activities and issue IDs.
- Preserve original photographs and metadata.
- Confirm verbal direction in writing.
- Document both the event and its actual effect.
- Record mitigation and work available during a delay.
- Do not silently alter approved records.
- Remove superseded documents from field use while preserving history.
- Protect personal, medical, security and privileged information.
- Audit record completeness throughout the project.
- Make final records searchable and retrievable.
