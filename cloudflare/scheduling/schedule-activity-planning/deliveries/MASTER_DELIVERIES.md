3. Finish selection and field verification
4. Release for fabrication
5. Procurement and manufacturing
6. Factory testing
7. Ready to ship and freight booked
8. Departure/customs/tracking
9. Delivery appointment/logistics/crane approval
10. Receipt, quantity and condition inspection
11. Acceptance/rejection, storage and protection
12. Installation, startup, testing and closeout spares
