# SSX Master Design and Engineering Activities

Potential construction schedule activities for planning, developing, coordinating, reviewing, issuing and maintaining architectural, engineering, delegated-design and field-design information from programming through record documents.

> Select only activities required by the project delivery method, contract, design stage, permits, delegated-design scope and construction needs. Design responsibility and approval authority must remain consistent with the contract and applicable professional-licensing requirements.

## Core design questions

| Field | Required answer |
|---|---|
| What is required? | Decision, study, calculation, drawing, specification, model, detail, response, approval or release |
| Why is it needed? | Design development, permit, procurement, coordination, fabrication, construction, testing, commissioning or closeout |
| Where does it apply? | Site, building, floor, room, grid, elevation, assembly, system or equipment |
| Who authors it? | Architect, engineer, consultant, delegated designer, contractor, subcontractor, supplier or specialty engineer |
| Who reviews it? | Design lead, consultants, contractor, owner, utility, commissioning authority, third party or AHJ |
| When is it needed? | Date calculated from procurement, fabrication, installation, inspection or milestone |
| What are the inputs? | Owner decision, survey, investigation, criteria, product data, calculations, coordination or field measurement |
| What releases? | Permit submission, procurement, fabrication, construction, testing, turnover or occupancy |
| What proves completion? | Approved deliverable, issued revision, transmittal, model publication, permit approval or field verification |

## Typical schedule logic

- Owner criteria → programming approval
- Surveys and investigations → design basis
- Design basis → schematic design
- Schematic approval → design development
- Design-development approval → construction documents
- Coordinated documents → permit submission
- Permit comments → revisions
- Permit approval and owner approval → issue for construction
- Design release → submittals and procurement
- Field question → RFI response
- Approved design change → revised construction
- Installed work and redlines → record documents
- Record-document approval → final closeout

## Required schedule controls

- Link design deliverables to the construction and procurement activities they release.
- Calculate need-by dates backward from field requirements.
- Track authoring, review, revision and approval as separate activities.
- Identify all required owner decisions and external approvals.
- Preserve design-package boundaries and interfaces.
- Control revisions and remove superseded documents.
- Treat delegated design as real design work with inputs, calculations, review and approval.
- Include field verification before finalizing dimension-dependent design.
- Connect RFIs and revisions to change management.
- Track BIM coordination to measurable area or system completion.
- Include permit resubmittal time when designs change.
- Update record documents continuously rather than waiting until project end.
- Preserve a traceable record of issued and approved information.
