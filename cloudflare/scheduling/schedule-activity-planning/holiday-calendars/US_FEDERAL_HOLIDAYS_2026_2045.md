# SSX United States Holiday Calendars — 2026–2045

**Purpose:** Twenty-year holiday reference for SSX construction schedules, organized by holiday year.

## Calendar rules

- The base calendar contains the 11 federal holidays established by U.S. law.
- Dates shown in the first column are the standard observed dates for a Monday-through-Friday workweek.
- When a fixed-date federal holiday falls on Saturday, the observed date is the preceding Friday.
- When a fixed-date federal holiday falls on Sunday, the observed date is the following Monday.
- The actual calendar date is retained when it differs from the observed date.
- Private construction projects are not automatically closed on federal holidays. Confirm the contract, collective-bargaining agreements, project work rules, owner restrictions, subcontractor calendars and local practice.
- The day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve and New Year’s Eve are included as planning flags only. They are not federal holidays and should be activated only when the project calendar requires them.
- State, territorial, municipal, tribal, religious, cultural and company-specific holidays require project or jurisdiction add-ons.
- Presidential Inauguration Day is not included in the nationwide base calendar because it applies only to certain federal employees in the Washington, DC, metropolitan area.

## SSX calendar classifications

- Federal Holiday — Observed
- Federal Holiday — Actual Date
- Common Construction Shutdown — Optional
- State or Local Holiday — Project Add-on
- Union or Labor Agreement Holiday — Project Add-on
- Owner or Company Holiday — Project Add-on
- Religious or Cultural Holiday — Planning Awareness
- Weather or Emergency Closure — Project Event

## Schedule implementation guidance

- Load the observed dates as nonwork days only when required by the project’s assigned work calendar.
- Keep actual and observed dates linked so weekend field operations are handled correctly.
- Create separate calendars for office review work, field work, six-day workweeks, seven-day work, utility work, AHJ inspections and owner operations.
- Do not assume design teams, permit offices, testing agencies, suppliers and field crews use identical holiday calendars.
- Add a schedule note when a holiday affects deliveries, inspections, concrete placement, shutdowns, testing, startup or critical-path work.
- Review future calendars annually because laws, executive orders, contracts and company policies can change.
