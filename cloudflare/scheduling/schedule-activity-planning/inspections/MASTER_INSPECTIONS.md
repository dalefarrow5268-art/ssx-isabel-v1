# SSX Master Construction Project Inspection List

**Purpose:** Quick-reference inspection library for construction scheduling and project controls.  
**Last reviewed:** August 12, 2026  
**Scope:** Potential inspections, observations, tests, certifications, commissioning activities, and authority approvals that may arise on a U.S. commercial construction project.

> Requirements vary by jurisdiction, adopted code edition, occupancy, construction type, permit conditions, contract documents, and the project-specific Statement of Special Inspections. Confirm every project's requirements with its AHJ, design professionals, testing agency, commissioning authority, utilities, and owner.

## Recommended SSX tracking fields

For each applicable inspection, track:

- Project phase and CSI division
- Inspection name and category
- Governing authority or specification
- Responsible requester
- Required inspector, agency, or competent person
- Prerequisite work and required documents
- Required notice period
- Hold point / work that cannot be concealed
- Scheduled date and actual date
- Result: pending, passed, passed with comments, failed, or canceled
- Deficiencies and corrective actions
- Reinspection requirement and date
- Report, certificate, photographs, and attachments
- Cost responsibility
- Whether it blocks successor work, occupancy, or closeout
