# SSX Master Construction Safety Activities

Includes inspections, meetings, permits, checks, audits and drills. A toolbox talk is a Safety Meeting, not an inspection.

## Recurring Project Safety Activities

- daily superintendent walk
- daily safety inspection
- daily subcontractor area inspection
- daily pre-task/JHA review
- stretch and flex
- housekeeping/access/weather review
- weekly toolbox talk
- weekly documented inspection
- weekly coordination meeting
- monthly audit/committee/scorecard
- quarterly corporate audit
- owner/insurance/OSHA consultation audit
- follow-up reinspection
