# SSX Master Security and Site Access Activities

Potential construction schedule activities for protecting the site, controlling access, managing credentials, monitoring restricted areas, responding to incidents and transferring security systems from mobilization through occupancy.

> Select activities applicable to the project risk, owner requirements, occupied operations and contract. Coordinate security with life safety, emergency access, privacy, cybersecurity, public protection and applicable law.

## Core security questions

| Field | Required answer |
|---|---|
| What is protected? | People, property, materials, equipment, information, occupied operations or critical infrastructure |
| Where? | Perimeter, gate, building, floor, room, roof, laydown, office, utility area or digital system |
| From what risk? | Unauthorized entry, theft, vandalism, violence, trespass, sabotage, information loss or operational disruption |
| Who may enter? | Worker, visitor, owner, inspector, delivery driver, emergency responder or authorized specialist |
| When? | Work hours, after hours, weekends, shutdowns, high-risk operations, turnover or occupancy |
| How is access controlled? | Guard, badge, roster, key, lock, escort, camera, alarm, turnstile or digital credential |
| Who responds? | Security, superintendent, owner, police, fire department, emergency team or system administrator |
| What proves control? | Approved plan, access log, credential record, patrol log, video, incident report or turnover receipt |

## Standard security workflow

1. Assess site and operational risks
2. Define security zones and authority
3. Prepare security and access plan
4. Coordinate emergency and owner requirements
5. Install perimeter, gates, lighting and monitoring
6. Establish screening and credential procedures
7. Activate access and guard coverage
8. Monitor, patrol and audit
9. Report and investigate incidents
10. Modify controls as construction phases
11. Commission permanent security systems
12. Transfer control to owner
13. Recover credentials and remove temporary systems
14. Archive required records

## Typical schedule logic

- Risk assessment → security plan
- Security plan → perimeter and systems
- Screening and orientation → credential issue
- Credential activation → site access
- Phase change → revised access and security
- Permanent-system installation → testing
- Testing and training → owner security transfer
- Badge/key recovery and temporary-system removal → security closeout

## Required schedule controls

- Establish security before unrestricted mobilization and material delivery.
- Keep emergency access available regardless of security level.
- Link credentials to screening, orientation and current work assignment.
- Assign access by zone and time rather than granting unnecessary universal access.
- Audit active badges, keys and accounts regularly.
- Relocate cameras, lighting and guards as workfronts change.
- Protect privacy and confidential screening results.
- Preserve incident evidence under controlled access.
- Integrate door hardware, access control, life safety and owner operations.
- Recover credentials and deactivate digital access promptly.
- Schedule permanent-system testing, training and transfer before occupancy.
- Remove temporary security and restore affected property at closeout.
