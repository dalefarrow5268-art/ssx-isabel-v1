# SSX Master Construction Startup and Commissioning Activities

Potential commissioning planning, readiness, checkout, startup, testing, balancing, functional performance, integrated systems, training, documentation, seasonal and warranty activities that may affect a construction project schedule.

> Use only activities required by the contract documents, Owner’s Project Requirements, Basis of Design, commissioning plan, codes, energy requirements, AHJ, utilities, manufacturers and project-specific systems.

## Recommended SSX tracking fields

For every applicable startup or commissioning activity, track:

- Project, building, phase, floor, area and system
- Equipment tag, asset ID and specification section
- Activity type and test procedure
- Responsible contractor, vendor and technician
- Commissioning authority, design professional, owner and AHJ witnesses
- Required manufacturer representative
- Prerequisites and readiness criteria
- Required temporary or permanent utilities
- Approved submittals and control sequences
- Planned and actual start and finish
- Required stabilization, trend or run duration
- Notice and witness requirements
- Test instruments and calibration status
- Acceptance criteria and measured results
- Deficiencies, responsible party and due date
- Retest requirement and date
- Status and approval
- Linked installation, inspection, TAB, training and turnover activities
- Report, checklist, certificate, trend and attachment
- Warranty start and seasonal follow-up dates

## Common hold points

- No startup before readiness requirements are satisfied
- No TAB before systems are complete, clean and controllable
- No functional testing before startup, pre-functional checks, controls and TAB
- No integrated testing before individual systems pass
- No owner acceptance before critical deficiencies are resolved
- No final commissioning report before deferred items are identified and assigned
- No removal of temporary test provisions before required testing is complete
- No warranty closeout before seasonal and deferred tests are resolved

## Required schedule controls

- Integrate commissioning activities into the construction schedule, not a separate disconnected list.
- Schedule design review, submittal review, checklist development and test-procedure approval early.
- Separate installation completion, startup, TAB, controls, functional testing and integrated testing.
- Include correction and retest time.
- Track each system and major equipment tag.
- Use defined readiness gates before scheduling witnesses.
- Protect required test ports, access and temporary provisions.
- Link commissioning issues to responsible construction activities.
- Complete owner training before operational turnover.
- Carry seasonal and deferred testing beyond occupancy when necessary.
- Align warranty reviews before contractual warranty expiration.
- Preserve complete reports, trends, calibration and acceptance evidence.
