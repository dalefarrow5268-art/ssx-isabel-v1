# SSX Master Sustainability and Green-Building Compliance Activities

Potential construction schedule activities for planning, documenting, implementing, testing and certifying sustainability requirements from design through post-occupancy.

> Select activities required by the contract, adopted code, owner goals and the specific rating system and version. Certification rules change; the project’s registered program documents and responsible sustainability professional govern.

## Core sustainability questions

| Field | Required answer |
|---|---|
| What requirement applies? | Code, owner standard, rating-system prerequisite, credit, performance target or reporting obligation |
| Where? | Site, material, building, floor, room, system, work package or supply chain |
| Who owns it? | Owner, designer, contractor, subcontractor, supplier, commissioning authority or consultant |
| When is evidence needed? | Design review, submittal, procurement, delivery, installation, testing, turnover or performance period |
| What action earns compliance? | Selection, calculation, protection, segregation, testing, commissioning, training or reporting |
| What evidence is required? | Product declaration, certificate, invoice, waste ticket, photograph, test result, model, narrative or signed form |
| What threatens compliance? | Late selection, unavailable product, missing documentation, substitution, contamination, failed testing or incomplete commissioning |
| How is it closed? | Reviewed evidence, resolved comments, submitted package, certification decision or documented owner acceptance |

2. Establish responsibility and scorecard
3. Identify prerequisites and critical credits
4. Integrate requirements into design and specifications
5. Establish submittal and procurement controls
6. Train project and trade teams
7. Collect product and supplier evidence
8. Implement site, waste and IAQ plans
9. Inspect construction compliance
10. Test and commission performance
11. Resolve missing or rejected evidence
12. Submit certification documentation
13. Respond to review comments
14. Complete owner training and turnover
15. Track post-occupancy performance when required

## Typical schedule logic

- Sustainability goals → scorecard and responsibility
- Scorecard → design and specification requirements
- Approved sustainable product → procurement
- Delivery → documentation and receiving verification
- Construction controls → inspections and records
- System completion → commissioning and testing
- Completed evidence → certification submission
- Review comments → revisions
- Final review → certification
- Turnover → post-occupancy performance

## Required schedule controls

- Identify the exact rating system and version.
- Schedule prerequisites before optional credits.
- Link documentation need-by dates to procurement releases.
- Verify evidence before purchasing products that depend on credit eligibility.
- Track substitutions for sustainability impact.
- Collect waste tickets and product evidence continuously.
- Schedule IAQ, moisture and pollution controls before affected work.
- Include testing, commissioning, flush-out and review durations.
- Maintain buffer credits where certification risk warrants.
- Assign one accountable owner to every requirement.
- Preserve a searchable audit trail.
- Transfer ongoing performance duties to the owner.
