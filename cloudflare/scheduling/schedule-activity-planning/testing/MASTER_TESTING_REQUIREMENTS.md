# SSX Master Construction Testing Requirements

Potential field, laboratory, manufacturer, qualification, pressure, load, performance, acceptance, commissioning and warranty tests for U.S. construction projects.

> Select only tests required by the plans, specifications, approved submittals, Statement of Special Inspections, adopted codes, AHJ, utilities, owner, manufacturer and commissioning plan.

## Preconstruction and Existing Conditions

- Concrete core/compressive-strength, petrographic, carbonation, chloride, ultrasonic, rebound-hammer and impact-echo testing
- Ground-penetrating radar and reinforcing-steel scanning
- Existing masonry unit, prism and mortar testing
- Structural steel coupon and weld nondestructive testing
- Wood moisture, species, decay and fungal testing
- Lead, asbestos, mold, radon, soil and groundwater testing
- Existing sewer camera, water-quality, hydrant-flow, electrical and HVAC testing
- Baseline noise, vibration, settlement and indoor-air-quality testing

## Geotechnical and Earthwork

- Soil classification, moisture, grain-size, sieve, hydrometer and Atterberg limits
- Proctor, relative density, CBR, R-value, shear, consolidation, swell, collapse and permeability testing
- Soil pH, sulfate, chloride, corrosivity, thermal/electrical resistivity and organic-content testing
- Field density by nuclear gauge, sand cone, balloon or drive cylinder
- Proof-roll, dynamic cone penetrometer, plate-load and lightweight deflectometer testing
- Fill, trench backfill, subgrade, retaining-wall backfill and stabilization verification

## Deep Foundations and Anchors

- Static compression, tension and lateral pile load tests
- Dynamic pile, PDA, low/high-strain integrity and crosshole sonic logging
- Thermal integrity profiling and drilled-shaft slurry testing
- Micropile, helical-pile, tieback, soil-nail and rock-anchor proof/performance/creep tests
- Post-installed and adhesive anchor tension, torque and proof-load testing

## Concrete

- Mix qualification, trial batch, cement, aggregate, admixture and mixing-water testing
- Temperature, slump/slump flow, air content, unit weight, yield and batch verification
- Cylinder/beam fabrication and compressive, flexural, tensile and early-age strength testing
- Field-cured cylinders, maturity, pullout, penetration, rebound and concrete core testing
- Shrinkage, creep, modulus, chloride permeability, resistivity, freeze-thaw and air-void testing
- Floor flatness/levelness, moisture-vapor emission, relative humidity, pH, bond and coating adhesion testing
- Shotcrete qualification panels, cores and nozzleman testing

## Post-Tensioning and Precast

- Jack calibration, stressing force, tendon elongation, friction and seating-loss testing
- Grout flow, fluidity, bleed and compressive-strength testing
- Duct leakage, vacuum-grouting and tendon watertightness verification
- Precast release strength and member load testing

## Masonry

- Unit strength, absorption, dimensions and density
- Mortar aggregate ratio, strength, air, water retention and consistency
- Grout slump/strength and masonry prism/core testing
- Bond-wrench, shear, anchor pullout, veneer tie and water-penetration testing

## Structural Steel and Metals

- Steel chemical, tensile, yield, bend and Charpy testing
- Welder/operator and welding-procedure qualification
- Visual, magnetic-particle, liquid-penetrant, ultrasonic, phased-array and radiographic weld testing
- Bolt rotational capacity, pretension, Skidmore, torque and direct-tension-indicator verification
- Shear-stud bend/ring tests
- Coating thickness, holiday, adhesion and galvanizing tests
- Member, railing, guard and crane runway load tests

## Wood and Mass Timber

- Moisture, species, grade, preservative retention/penetration and fire-retardant testing
- Adhesive bond, glulam, finger-joint, delamination and structural composite lumber tests
- Truss/shear-wall proof loads and fastener/connector pull tests
- Mass-timber moisture, bond, acoustic and fire-resistance testing

## Fireproofing and Rated Construction

- Sprayed fireproofing thickness, density and adhesion/cohesion
- Intumescent dry-film thickness, adhesion and holiday testing
- Firestop destructive, adhesion and depth verification
- Fire-resistive joint movement and perimeter containment testing
- Fire/smoke door leakage, drop and operational testing
- Fire/smoke damper and fire-curtain operational testing

## Roofing, Waterproofing and Envelope

- Roof substrate moisture, membrane seam peel/shear/weld and fastener pullout testing
- Roof wind uplift, vacuum, flood, electronic leak detection and infrared moisture survey
- Below-grade/plaza/balcony/shower/pool/tank waterproofing adhesion, thickness, holiday and flood tests
- Laboratory/field curtain-wall structural, air, static/dynamic water, thermal and drift testing
- Window, door, storefront and skylight air/water/structural testing
- Air-barrier adhesion, thickness, smoke continuity, whole-building leakage and thermography
- Sealant adhesion, cladding pullout/bond, masonry spray and wall drainage testing

## Interior Materials

- Concrete moisture, RH, pH and vapor-emission testing
- Flooring/tile/coating/paint/wall-covering adhesion and slip-resistance testing
- Partition, operable partition, ceiling and room acoustical testing
- Raised-floor, guard, handrail, casework and athletic-floor load/performance testing

## Pavement and Site

- Aggregate gradation, abrasion and soundness
- Asphalt mix, binder, gradation, temperature, density, air void, core thickness and smoothness
- Concrete paving slump, air, strength, thickness and dowel alignment
- Pavement deflection, skid resistance and drainage testing

## Underground Utilities

- Sewer air/water, infiltration/exfiltration, mandrel, deflection and camera testing
- Manhole vacuum/leak testing and storm-pipe testing
- Water/fire main hydrostatic, flushing, disinfection, bacteriological and flow testing
- Gas/fuel piping pressure and leak testing
- Duct-bank mandrel, conduit continuity/proofing and tracer-wire testing
- Cathodic protection, detention infiltration and stormwater functional testing

## Plumbing, Gas and Medical Gas

- Domestic water pressure/flow, flushing, disinfection, potability and Legionella testing
- Hot-water temperature/return balancing and fixture flow testing
- Sanitary, waste/vent, storm and roof-drain testing
- Backflow, cross-connection, interceptor, sump, ejector, pump and heater functional testing
- Fuel/process/steam/compressed-air piping hydrostatic, pneumatic, purity, cleaning and valve tests
- Medical-gas pressure, leakage, blowdown, particulate, purity, cross-connection, alarm and identity testing

## HVAC and Mechanical

- Duct pressure/leakage, grease-duct leakage, cleanliness and light-leak testing
- Airflow/TAB, room pressure, air changes, stair/shaft pressurization and smoke visualization
- Kitchen hood capture, laboratory fume hood, HEPA scan and filter leak testing
- Fan, pump and rotating-equipment vibration/sound testing
- Hydronic/refrigerant pressure, flushing, treatment, glycol, balance and flow testing
- Boiler combustion/efficiency/emissions, chiller/cooling tower/heat exchanger performance
- Refrigerant leak, vacuum decay, evacuation, charge, detector and machinery-room testing

## Building Automation

- Point-to-point, sensor calibration, actuator stroke and control-loop testing
- Sequences, alarms, trends, schedules, resets, staging and lead-lag testing
- Economizer, demand ventilation, energy recovery, smoke/fire interfaces and restart testing
- BACnet/network interoperability, metering, graphics, cybersecurity and failover testing

## Electrical

- Continuity, insulation resistance, megger, hi-pot/VLF, phasing and cable tests
- Transformer ratio, winding resistance, power factor, oil and dissolved-gas tests
- Switchgear contact/insulation, breaker primary/secondary injection and timing
- Protective relay, coordination, ground-fault and arc-energy-reduction testing
- Grounding/bonding, ground resistance, busway, motor/VFD and torque testing
- Power quality, harmonics, phase balance, voltage drop, load and infrared thermography

## Emergency Power, PV, Batteries and EV

- Generator factory/startup, load-bank, step-load, full-load, fuel, sound and emissions testing
- ATS, paralleling, load shed, blackout, duration and emergency-lighting testing
- UPS and battery capacity, impedance, conductance and runtime testing
- PV continuity, polarity, insulation, voltage/current, ground/arc fault, rapid shutdown and production tests
- Battery storage capacity/BMS/thermal detection/shutdown tests and EV charger/load-management tests

## Fire Protection and Life Safety

- Fire-main, sprinkler and standpipe hydrostatic, flushing, flow, main-drain and trip testing
- Fire-pump churn, rated flow, 150% flow, controller, auto-start and emergency-power testing
- Fire-alarm device, circuit, notification, voice intelligibility, monitoring and secondary-power testing
- Elevator recall/shunt, door release/unlock, HVAC shutdown and smoke-control interfaces
- Clean-agent enclosure integrity, retention, release, abort and shutdown testing
- Foam, water mist, dry/wet chemical and kitchen suppression testing
- Smoke-control pressure, airflow, damper, visualization and integrated sequence testing
- Emergency responder radio and mass-notification acceptance testing

## Elevators and Conveying

- Rated/no-load, governor, safety, buffer, brake, door, speed, leveling and ride tests
- Fire service, emergency power, communication, hydraulic and battery-lowering tests
- Escalator, platform lift, material lift and dumbwaiter load/safety tests

## Technology, Security and AV

- Copper/fiber certification, OTDR, polarity, network throughput/latency and Wi-Fi coverage
- DAS/public-safety radio coverage, grounding, redundancy and failover testing
- Access control, door alarm/release, intrusion, duress, video coverage/retention and cybersecurity testing
- Audio frequency/SPL/intelligibility, video calibration, conferencing and emergency override testing

## Specialty Systems

- Kitchen exhaust/makeup air/suppression, refrigeration pull-down and sanitization tests
- Pool shell/piping/turnover/filter/chemistry/entrapment/bonding tests
- Healthcare pressure, HEPA, particle, cleanroom, pharmacy, medical, shielding, MRI and sterilizer tests
- Industrial process FAT/SAT, vessel, gas detection, explosion, dust collection and safety-interlock tests

## Environmental, Energy and Sustainability

- Stormwater turbidity, pH, suspended solids, discharge, groundwater and contamination testing
- Asbestos/lead/mold clearance, radon, dust, noise, vibration and IAQ testing
- Whole-building air, duct, HVAC controls, lighting controls, receptacles and metering functional tests
- LEED/WELL air, water, lighting, acoustic, thermal comfort and performance verification

## Commissioning, Acceptance and Warranty

- Factory and site acceptance testing
- Pre-functional, startup and functional performance testing
- Failure-mode, redundancy, switchover, network/power loss and system recovery testing
- Integrated systems, seasonal, deferred and opposite-season testing
- AHJ/utility/owner witnessed acceptance and retesting
- 30-day through one-year warranty, roof moisture, infrared, water, IAQ and energy follow-up testing

## SSX test classifications

- Qualification, mockup, factory acceptance, source QC, field QC, destructive, nondestructive, proof, pressure, leakage, load, startup, functional, performance, acceptance, integrated, commissioning, diagnostic, retest, seasonal, deferred, warranty and recurring operational test.

## Required tracking

Track governing standard, responsible trade, testing agency, sample/area/frequency, prerequisites, cure/stabilization time, calibration, required witness, notice period, pass criteria, planned/actual date, report turnaround, result, deficiency, corrective work, retest and affected successor work.
