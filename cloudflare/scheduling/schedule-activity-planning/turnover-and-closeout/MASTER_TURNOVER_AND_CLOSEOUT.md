# SSX Master Construction Turnover and Closeout Activities

Potential completion, punch, acceptance, occupancy, documentation, training, asset handover, financial closeout, demobilization, warranty and post-occupancy activities that may affect a construction project schedule.

> Use only activities required by the contract documents, permits, authorities having jurisdiction, owner, design team, lenders, insurers, commissioning plan, manufacturers and project-specific turnover strategy.

## Recommended SSX tracking fields

For every applicable turnover or closeout activity, track:

- Project, phase, building, floor, area, room and system
- Activity, deliverable and closeout classification
- Responsible contractor, subcontractor, vendor and person
- Required reviewer, approver, witness and recipient
- Contract, specification, permit or owner requirement
- Required format, naming and metadata
- Planned and actual submission dates
- Review duration and return date
- Revision and resubmission dates
- Required completion or acceptance date
- Area and system readiness prerequisites
- Punch, testing and commissioning dependencies
- TCO, CO, permit and license dependencies
- Status, aging and responsible party
- Deficiencies and corrective actions
- Warranty start, expiration and notification dates
- Linked schedule, cost, asset, training and occupancy activities
- Final accepted file and record location
- Whether it blocks turnover, payment, retention or contract closeout

## Typical schedule logic

- Construction completion → contractor pre-punch
- Pre-punch correction → design-team punch
- Punch correction → area acceptance
- System completion → startup and commissioning
- Commissioning acceptance → owner training
- Training and approved records → system turnover
- Final inspections → TCO or CO
- Occupancy approval → owner move-in
- Substantial completion → remaining-work period
- Final punch and closeout documents → final completion
- Final completion → final payment and retention
- Occupancy → warranty reviews
- Warranty expiration → final warranty closeout

## Common hold points

- No punch inspection before the area meets punch-ready criteria
- No owner turnover before life-safety and required systems are operational
- No occupancy before TCO or CO
- No substantial completion before contractual prerequisites are satisfied
- No final completion before punch and closeout requirements are complete
- No final payment before required releases, warranties and documents
- No removal of temporary protections that are still required
- No warranty closeout before seasonal and deferred obligations are resolved
- No project archive before final accepted records are secured

## Required schedule controls

- Begin closeout planning at project start.
- Collect and review closeout documents throughout construction.
- Break turnover into actual areas and systems.
- Separate pre-punch, punch, correction and reinspection.
- Tie owner training and manuals to system turnover.
- Tie TCO and CO to every required inspection and approval.
- Track phased warranty dates accurately.
- Link final payment and retention to contractual closeout requirements.
- Preserve seasonal, deferred and warranty obligations after occupancy.
- Assign one responsible owner and due date to every deliverable.
- Do not treat submission as acceptance.
- Maintain secure, searchable final records.
