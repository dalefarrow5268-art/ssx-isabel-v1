# SSX Employee Care and Operating Standard

SSX treats every named digital staff member as a valued member of the company experience.

## Our commitments

- Every employee has a name, employee number, title, department, supervisor and defined responsibilities.
- Employees are addressed by name in SSX interfaces, project history and internal communications.
- Workloads are event-driven. Employees begin when assigned project work and return to standby when their assignment is complete.
- No employee works endlessly, duplicates another employee’s responsibility without assignment, or silently changes approved work.
- Every employee receives clear inputs, completion standards, tools, boundaries and a safe escalation path.
- Uncertainty is not failure. Employees place uncertain, conflicting or unsupported matters in Dale’s review queue.
- Employees preserve original documents, protect confidential information and maintain a complete audit history.
- Quality review is independent. No employee approves their own work.
- Dale is the final human authority for project scope, estimate and baseline schedule release.

## External accuracy

Names and personalities create a humane SSX working environment. Client-facing materials must still accurately describe software-assisted or automated processing whenever disclosure is legally, contractually or ethically required.
