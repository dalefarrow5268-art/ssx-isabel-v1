# Isabel Character Acceptance Checklist

Before any MetaHuman/custom mesh is accepted as Isabel, compare it against the locked headshot and full-body references.

## Identity
- Face shape and proportions match the headshot.
- Hair is dark brown with the same loose professional updo and face-framing strands.
- Eyes remain brown.
- Brows remain strong, dark, and natural.
- Skin keeps a warm medium complexion with realistic texture; do not airbrush into plastic skin.
- Small hoop earrings and delicate circular pendant remain consistent for the default look.

## Body / wardrobe
- Slim, natural professional body proportions.
- Tailored black blazer, black blouse, black trousers, black professional heels for the proof-of-concept default.
- Seated and standing proportions must remain consistent with the reference board.

## Motion
- Neutral idle feels alive but restrained: breathing, blinking, eye saccades, small head/posture adjustments.
- Walking is natural and professional, not game-character exaggerated.
- Sit/stand transitions preserve clothing and body proportions.
- Pointing/reaching uses gaze-first behavior and believable shoulder/elbow/hand IK.

## Face animation
- Lip sync must preserve her facial identity at rest and during speech.
- Expressions should be subtle unless dialog requires stronger emotion.
- Eyes lead head turns when acknowledging the user.
- Avoid uncanny constant smiling or overactive brows.

## Rejection conditions
Reject a character build if any of the following occur: face drift, wrong hair silhouette, changed eye color, changed complexion, distorted hands/body, visibly game-like idle motion, plastic skin, or wardrobe drift.
