# Isabel World-Class Quality Bar

Isabel is not finished when she merely talks. She is finished only when she feels like a competent, persistent digital person who inhabits SSX and can act safely on real work.

## 1. Identity fidelity
- Same recognizable Isabel face across every camera angle and session.
- No hair, skin tone, eye, body-proportion, or wardrobe drift unless explicitly changed.
- Close-up facial quality must survive ordinary office lighting without uncanny smoothing.

## 2. Human timing
- Eyes lead head turns.
- Head/body movement follows speech intent, not random animation loops.
- Natural pauses, blinks, breathing, listening nods, and attention shifts.
- User interruption must work quickly and gracefully.

## 3. Speech quality
- Low perceived response latency.
- Audio is the timing truth for facial motion.
- Prosody must reflect meaning rather than generic enthusiasm.
- No audible clipping, repeated words, or delayed mouth motion.

## 4. Physical agency
- Isabel can sit, stand, walk, turn, stop, look, point, reach, and return to work without teleporting.
- Hands and feet respect surfaces and floor contact.
- Movement must resolve through named anchors and navigation, never raw LLM coordinates.

## 5. Persistent world
- Office architecture never regenerates when Isabel moves or speaks.
- Screens are independent live surfaces.
- Camera, desk, chair, windows, cabinetry, lighting, artwork, and monitor frames remain deterministic.
- Reconnect resumes a stable world state instead of resetting the experience.

## 6. Work competence
- Isabel can understand SSX data, evidence, schedule, risk, cameras, documents, and project state.
- She can choose whether to stay seated or physically move to supporting information.
- She explains why something matters, not just what it says.

## 7. Safety and authority
- External actions requiring human approval stay behind explicit authority boundaries.
- Isabel distinguishes facts, inferences, recommendations, and unknowns.
- Every consequential action leaves inspectable audit residue.

## 8. Visual quality
- Bright, credible office lighting with readable monitors.
- No watercolor softness, muddy cabinetry, bent window frames, blown whites, or distorted UI.
- Stable 16:9 monitor geometry and uniform reveal.

## 9. Reliability targets
- A single subsystem failure should degrade gracefully, not collapse the office.
- Screen/data outages show controlled fallback states.
- Health checks identify exact failed subsystem.
- Startup path should be repeatable from a clean reboot.

## 10. Competitive standard
We compare Isabel against the best parts of real-time avatar, virtual-human, game-character, AI-assistant, and digital-twin systems—but we do not copy their product constraints. Isabel should combine their strengths: realism, low latency, agency, persistent memory, spatial awareness, live tools/data, and inspectable authority.

## Release rule
If the system looks impressive in a screenshot but fails conversation, motion, persistence, screen fidelity, or safe work execution, it does not pass.
